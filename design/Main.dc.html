About

Awesheim is the illustration and design practice of Olav Aasheim. The work is mostly for heavy music — cover art, gatefolds, sigils and the layout that holds a record together. Drawn by hand, separated for print, and built to survive being shrunk to a thumbnail and blown up to a backdrop.
